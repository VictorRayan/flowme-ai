Privacy Policy & Google User Data Disclosures

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

At Flow Me (operated by CNPJ: 49.858.359/0001-93 - Rayan Barbieri Consultoria em Tecnologia), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our virtual assistant, Balys, and the Flow Me platform access, use, store, share, and protect your personal information, specifically focusing on the data accessed through Google API Services.

1. Google User Data Accessed (Scopes)

To deliver our core productivity features, Flow Me requests authorization to connect to your Google account. We only access data within the scopes you explicitly authorize, which may include:

  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar): Used to list, read, create, update, and delete calendar events on your behalf, keeping your schedule synchronized.
  • Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): Used to read email metadata (headers, subject, sender) and body contents so the assistant can search, read, and summarize messages for you.
  • Google Docs (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents): Used to list, read, edit, write, and create documents in your Google Docs account based on your instructions.
  • Google Sheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets): Used to list, read, and write cell values in your spreadsheets to help organize financial, task, or business records.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly): Used to search, locate, and read files and folders inside your Google Drive as requested by your commands.

2. Data Usage and Purpose

We use Google user data exclusively to power your personal assistant, Balys. Data is retrieved and processed only in response to your direct, user-initiated commands (such as asking "What is scheduled for tomorrow?", "Read my last email", or "Create a new document with my meeting notes").

The assistant processes your natural language inputs, searches your files or accounts internally, matches titles or search queries, and retrieves the relevant information to answer your questions or execute the requested task. We do not use this data for any marketing, advertising, or profiling purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

Flow Me values your trust. We implement strict constraints regarding how your data is shared:

  • AI API Service Providers: To understand and respond to your natural language queries, user commands and the text content retrieved from your Google account (such as the text inside a Doc, Sheet, or Email body) may be transmitted to secure AI model API endpoints (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini).
  • Zero Data Retention & No Model Training: Under our developer agreements with these AI providers, they are strictly prohibited from storing, retaining, or using any part of this user data to train or fine-tune their AI models.
  • No External Sharing: We do not share, sell, rent, lease, or distribute your Google user data with any other third parties, advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

4. Data Storage and Protection

We take the security of your data seriously and apply the following protective measures:

  • Secure Token Storage: Your Google OAuth credentials (access tokens and refresh tokens) are encrypted at rest and stored securely in our databases.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between Google APIs, our backend servers, and your clients (WhatsApp interface or the Web App dashboard) is fully encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
  • Access Isolation: We enforce strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) and app-level controls to ensure that only the authenticated account owner can trigger operations on their connected integrations.

5. Data Retention, Deletion, and Revocation

We keep your connected integration credentials only as long as your account remains active and the integration is connected.

  • Self-Service Disconnection: You can disconnect your Google account from Flow Me at any time through the Integrations dashboard in the web panel. Disconnecting instantly and permanently deletes your OAuth credentials and access tokens from our database.
  • Full Account Deletion: You can request the complete deletion of your Flow Me account and all stored history at any time. To request deletion, you can use the account settings inside the web application or contact us at suporte@flow-me.online. We will permanently delete all your data within 30 days.
  • Revoking Consent via Google Settings: You can also revoke Flow Me's access to your Google account at any time via the Google Security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Flow Me's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
